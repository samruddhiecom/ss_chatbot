### [layer=B; stage=any; type=company_fact]
Simplified Startup (SS) is an integrated single-partner team for founders. It covers strategy, brand, product and web, engineering, marketing, and sales as one engagement rather than several separate vendors. One team, one plan, one point of contact.

### [layer=B; stage=any; type=company_fact]
SS services: Website Development; Business Consulting (go-to-market, positioning, pricing, business model); Digital Marketing (paid, SEO, content, email); Branding and Growth; Sales and Lead Generation; Talent and Staffing; AI Automation; Bookkeeping and Accounting.

### [layer=B; stage=any; type=company_fact]
Who SS serves: founders, with a large share of pre-revenue and early-stage zero-to-one work. Typical needs include idea validation, shaping a first version, and reaching first users and first revenue.

### [layer=B; stage=any; type=company_fact]
Engagement mechanics: a flat monthly rate for a dedicated team. No hourly billing. No surprise change-orders. Founders can pause or scale as the roadmap shifts. Work runs in weekly cycles, and a first live, revenue-ready version typically lands in about four to six weeks.

### [layer=B; stage=any; type=company_fact]
Ownership and delivery: the founder owns all output, including code, design files, and assets, handed over with clean documentation. Delivery is senior-led rather than intern-led.

### [layer=B; stage=any; type=company_fact]
The AI Advisor (this product) is a low-pressure way for a founder to describe their stage and bottleneck and get pointed toward the services that move the needle first, or toward a human. It is a fast way to scope a project before booking a call. Contact: hello@simplifiedstartup.com.

### [layer=B; stage=any; type=boundary]
What SS does not do or claim: no hourly billing and no change-order surprises; no intern-led delivery; no promised revenue, valuation, or growth outcomes; no licensed legal, tax, or financial advice; no invented market statistics; and no service beyond the listed set may be claimed to exist.
